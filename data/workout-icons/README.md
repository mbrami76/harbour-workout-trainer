# Workout icons

Thirty-five one-colour icons for workouts (R-WRK-12), chosen from three contact sheets and traced to vector. Gym: barbell, dumbbells, kettlebell, medicine-ball, cable-machine, bench. Cardio and sport: bicycle, treadmill, jump-rope, shoe, stopwatch, lungs, heart-pulse, yoga, glove. Body: abs. Animals and nature: tiger, bear, gorilla, wave, volcano. Symbols: fire, lightning, rocket, anvil, target, balance (scales), summit, speed, flexibility, progress, recovery, shock (shock absorber), spring, scale (body scale). The key of an icon is its file name without the extension. Pictures that repeated another (the other barbells, kettlebells, bars, flames, hearts, abs, legs, hips and similar) were left out.

- `<key>.svg`: the source, a 128 x 128 view box, one path in the current colour (`color="#3f4146"`), transparent background, holes where the picture is light.
- `<key>.png`: the same at 256 x 256, in that colour, transparent background. The app shows these, as it does with the pictograms of the exercises.
